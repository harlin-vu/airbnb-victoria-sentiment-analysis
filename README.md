# Airbnb Victoria Customer Experience Analytics

Business Analytics | Natural Language Processing | Sentiment Analysis | Topic Modelling

## Overview

Understanding customer satisfaction is essential for improving Airbnb host performance and guest experience. This project analyses Airbnb listings and guest reviews across Victoria, Australia using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), VADER Sentiment Analysis, and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) to uncover customer preferences and identify opportunities for service improvement.

---

## Business Questions

This project addresses three key business questions:

- Which neighbourhoods achieve the highest guest satisfaction?
- What factors contribute to negative guest experiences?
- How do customer expectations differ between accommodation types?

---

## Methodology

- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- VADER Sentiment Analysis
- Negative Review Analysis
- LDA Topic Modelling
- Business Insight Generation

---

## Market Performance

<img src="Figure 1 – Number of Listings in top 10 neighbourhoods.png" width="750">

### Key Findings

- Melbourne has the highest concentration of Airbnb listings in Victoria.
- Yarra Ranges achieves the highest average guest satisfaction.
- High market activity does not necessarily translate into higher customer satisfaction.

---

## Drivers of Customer Dissatisfaction

<img src="Figure 5 – Average Sentiment by Negative Reviews.png" width="750">

### Key Findings

- Bathroom cleanliness is the most common source of negative reviews.
- Host responsiveness is another major contributor to customer dissatisfaction.
- Guest experience is influenced more by service quality than listing characteristics.

---

## Topic Modelling

<img src="Figure 8 - Topic Popularity.png" width="750">

### Key Findings

- Guests frequently discuss accessibility, room quality, and helpful hosts.
- These themes represent the most influential aspects of the overall Airbnb experience.

---

## Customer Expectations by Room Type

<img src="Figure 9 – Topic Distributions by Room Type.png" width="750">

### Key Findings

- Guests staying in **Private Rooms** value host interaction and hospitality.
- Guests booking **Entire Home/Apt** listings prioritise privacy, convenience, and location.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several recommendations were developed:

- Improve bathroom hygiene and property maintenance.
- Enhance host communication and response time.
- Provide accommodation-specific guidance for hosts.
- Highlight neighbourhood accessibility and nearby attractions in listing descriptions.
- Encourage guests to leave post-stay reviews to strengthen platform credibility.
